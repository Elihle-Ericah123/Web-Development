About Nicole's Fried Chicken

Nicole's Fried Chicken is a fictional fast-food restaurant located in Newtown C, Inanda, Durban, KwaZulu-Natal.

The restaurant specialises in freshly prepared fried chicken, burgers, wings, chips, wraps, family meals and cold beverages at affordable prices.

Our Mission

While delivering excellent customer service to the Inanda community, Nicole's Fried Chicken provides delicious, freshly prepared meals at affordable prices.

Our Vision

To become the preferred fast-food restaurant in Inanda by offering quality food, exceptional service and a convenient digital experience for customers.

Our Target Customers

  • Families living in Inanda and surrounding areas
  • Students
  • Working professionals
  • Tourists visiting the area
  • Customers looking for affordable takeaway meals
